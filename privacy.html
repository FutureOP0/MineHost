Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

1. Information We Collect

MineHost operates entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. The app does not require user accounts or registration.

2. Advertising

MineHost uses the Start.io (formerly StartApp) advertising SDK to serve ads. Start.io may collect certain device information for the purpose of delivering relevant advertisements, including:

  • Device type, manufacturer, and model
  • Operating system version
  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • App usage data

For details on Start.io's data practices, please review their Privacy Policy.

3. Network Tunneling

MineHost integrates playit.gg for optional public server tunneling. When you enable tunneling, your server traffic is routed through playit.gg's infrastructure. Please refer to playit.gg's privacy policy for their data handling practices.

4. Local Data Storage

All server files, configurations, and world data are stored locally on your Android device. MineHost does not back up or synchronize this data to any cloud service.

5. Permissions

MineHost requests the following Android permissions:

  • Internet Access: Required for downloading server software, serving ads, and enabling public tunneling.
  • Foreground Service: Required to keep the Minecraft server running in the background.
  • Wake Lock: Prevents the device from sleeping while a server is active.
  • Network State: Checks connectivity before performing network operations.

6. Children's Privacy

MineHost is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
